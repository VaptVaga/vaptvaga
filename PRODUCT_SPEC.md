# VaptVaga (Versão Beta) - Product Specification

## 1. Concept
A "Mobile-First" SaaS marketplace connecting local physical businesses (restaurants, shops) with operational freelancers (waiters, cleaners, couriers) for quick shifts and urgent daily work. Focus on speed and reducing friction ("Candidatura em 1 Clique").

## 2. Business Model (Beta)
Freemium SaaS model. Monetization via monthly subscriptions to unlock limits (no native transaction splits in Beta).

### Freelancer (Supply)
- **Free (R$ 0/mês)**: Access to job feed, limit of **2 applications per month**, native chat only with matched companies.
- **Vapt PRO (R$ 14,90/mês)**: Unlimited applications, "Freela PRO" badge, highlighted in searches.

### Company (Demand)
- **Free (R$ 0/mês)**: Post 1 job per month, view all applicants, but **Native Chat unlocked with only 1 candidate**.
- **Negócio Ágil (R$ 49,90/mês)**: Unlimited job posts, Native Chat unlocked with ALL candidates, priority support.

## 3. Core Features
- **Dual Profile Creation**: Company or Freelancer.
- **Freelancer Dashboard (Job Feed)**: Clean cards (Title, Company, Pay, Date, Location). CTA: "Candidatura em 1 Clique".
- **Company Dashboard (Job Panel)**: "Publicar Urgência" form. View applicants with photo and rating.
- **Native In-App Chat**: Direct messaging to retain users in the ecosystem.
- **Paywall Triggers**: Interactive modals that block Free users when limits are hit (3rd application, 2nd chat).

## 4. Design System & UX
- **Mobile-First**: Must feel like a native app (bottom navigation, smooth transitions).
- **Visuals**: Clean, white/light gray backgrounds, dark text. Avoid clutter.
- **CTAs**: Bright Electric Blue or Mint Green.
- **Desktop**: Primarily for company managers using sidebars/split-screens.
